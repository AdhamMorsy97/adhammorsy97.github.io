2. The short version

Glassbrew has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress is saved only on your device. Ads and analytics start only after you have made a choice in the consent form, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. Purchases are processed by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

3. Data stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, coins, purchases and reminder choices are saved in a file on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

4. Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

When you first start the game in the EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or a region with similar rules, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested and before any analytics data is sent. Your choice is stored on your device by Google's User Messaging Platform (IAB Transparency and Consent Framework). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR together with § 25 TDDDG for storing the choice.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

The game shows ads between levels and optional reward ads that you choose to watch. AdMob processes device data such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language and ad interactions. With your consent ads may be personalised; without it, only limited, non-personalised ads are shown where Google allows this. Buying "Remove Ads" stops ads between levels; reward ads remain available only if you choose them.

Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

6. Game analytics and remote settings (GameAnalytics)

Provider: GameAnalytics ApS, Copenhagen, Denmark.

Only after your consent, the game sends anonymous gameplay events (for example level started or completed, moves, boosters used, ad and purchase events without payment data) together with device data (model, operating system, language, country derived from the IP address) and a random ID created by the game. We use this to improve difficulty, find bugs and tune game settings remotely. We do not send your name, email or any content you create.

Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. GameAnalytics privacy policy: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy

Retention: {{ANALYTICS_RETENTION}} (set this to the retention configured in GameAnalytics).

7. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms. We receive only a purchase token, the order ID and the product ID so we can deliver and restore your items. We never receive your payment details.

Legal basis: performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

8. Crash and performance data

Google Play may collect crash and performance reports (Android vitals) according to your device settings. They do not identify you to us.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a stable app, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

9. Notifications and sharing

Reminder notifications are created on your device only if you allow them; no data is sent for this. Sharing a Daily Brew result opens your system's share sheet; the text and image go only to the app you choose.

10. Children

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

11. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR), and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Settings > Privacy options). You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live.

12. Requesting or deleting data

Because there is no account, we cannot link data to your name. To request access to or deletion of analytics data, email adhammoursy.games@proton.me. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. Advertising data is controlled by Google; you can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings.

13. Changes

We update this policy when the game or the law changes. The current version is always in the game (Settings > Privacy Policy) and at https://adhammorsy97.github.io/glassbrew/privacy.html.

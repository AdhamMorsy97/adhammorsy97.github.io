Blind Spot Studio Uploader

A private publishing tool for two YouTube channels owned by one creator.

What it does

The tool runs on its owner's own computer. It uploads finished videos and thumbnails to the owner's channels, Blind Spot and Logic Explained, and schedules when each video goes public. It also reads each channel's list of scheduled videos so two videos are never booked on the same day.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It is not offered to the public, there is no sign-up, and nobody else's account is ever connected.

Data

  • Google sign-in is used only to act on the owner's own channels through the YouTube Data API.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never shared or sold.
